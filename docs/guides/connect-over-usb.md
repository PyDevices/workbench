# Connect a board over USB

Plug the board in and connect to it from Workbench over Web Serial (or WebUSB). You need a Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave): this isn't available on iOS or Safari. See [Browser and platform support](../reference/browser-support.md).

1. Connect your device with a USB cable and check that your operating system recognizes it. Some USB devices need a driver installed. Some boards aren't detected by Android over USB OTG.
2. Open [Workbench](https://pydevices.github.io/workbench/) and press the USB button.
3. Pick your board from the list.

If Android doesn't see your board, turn on `Use WebUSB instead of WebSerial` in Settings and try again.
